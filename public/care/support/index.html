Common questions

  • How does my nanny join? From Settings (or onboarding), send her the invite link. She opens it, sees her schedule, downloads the app, and signs in with Apple — no password to manage.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete account. When the last parent deletes, the household's data — including all photos — is deleted. Details in the Privacy Policy.
  • Does Sembli pay my nanny? No. Timesheets show hours × rate for your records; paying, taxes, and employment obligations stay between you and your nanny.
  • Is my family's data private? Yes — each household sees only its own data, photos are private to the household, and we never sell data or show ads.
