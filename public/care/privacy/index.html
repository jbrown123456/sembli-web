Privacy Policy

Effective July 10, 2026 · Terms of Service · Support

Sembli Care is an iOS app that helps a family (the "household") and the nanny they employ coordinate schedules, daily plans, tasks, weekly hours, and a private photo feed. This policy explains what personal information Sembli ("we," "us") collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Who uses the app, and whose data is involved

  • Parents/guardians create and manage a household account.
  • Nannies join a household by invitation and use the app for their work with that household.
  • Children do not use the app and cannot hold accounts. The app is for adults 18 and older. Children appear only as profiles created and controlled by the parent, who provides the child's information. See Section 6.

2. Information we collect

Provided by users:

  • Account and contact information: parent and nanny names, phone numbers, household name.
  • Child profiles (entered by the parent): child's name, birthdate, and optionally a photo, allergy information, and care notes.
  • Household coordination content: weekly schedules, daily plans, tasks, notes, and saved activities.
  • Timesheets: hours the nanny submits, the hourly rate the parent enters, and the resulting computed pay amounts. These are display math only — Sembli does not process, hold, or move money.
  • Photos: photos of children taken and shared by the nanny within the household feed, and any photo the parent adds to a child profile. Photos are private to the household.

Collected automatically:

  • Photo metadata: a subset of EXIF data from uploaded photos — timestamp, GPS location if present in the photo file, and device model. We do not otherwise track anyone's location, and we never track the nanny's real-time location.
  • Device push tokens so we can deliver notifications.
  • Minimal internal analytics: counts of feature usage collected by our own systems. No third-party analytics SDKs.

Authentication: Sign in with Apple (we receive the identifier and any name/email Apple shares per your settings) and invite-link tokens. We do not collect or store passwords.

What we do not collect: payment or bank details, government IDs, advertising identifiers, background-location or GPS tracking data, or data from any third-party ad or analytics SDK.

3. How we use information

  • Operate the service: schedules, plans, tasks, timesheets, the photo feed, and notifications.
  • Display timesheet math (hours × rate). This is informational only — not payroll processing or payroll, tax, or legal advice.
  • Translation and AI assistance: user-generated content is machine-translated between English and Spanish, and parents may use an AI assistant that drafts schedule changes and plans. Both are powered by the Anthropic API (Claude); see Section 4. Assistant-drafted changes require explicit parent confirmation before they are saved.
  • Secure the service, prevent abuse, and debug problems.
  • Understand aggregate feature usage to improve the app (internal analytics only).

We do not use your content or your children's data to train AI models, and we do not use any personal information for advertising.

5. Data location

Data is stored in the United States (AWS us-west-2).

6. Children's information

  • The app is intended for adults 18+; children are not users, do not hold accounts, and cannot sign in.
  • All child data (name, birthdate, photo, allergies, notes) is provided by the parent, who controls it. Photos of children shared by the nanny are visible only within that household.
  • Because children do not use the app and we collect no information directly from children online, the app is adult-directed and not a child-directed service.
  • Parents can edit or delete child profiles and photos at any time; deleting the household removes all child data (Section 8).

7. Retention

We keep personal information while the account and household are active. Timesheets and schedules are retained for the life of the household so families keep their records. When an account or household is deleted, data is removed as described in Section 8, except where a short backup-retention window applies or the law requires longer retention.

8. Deletion and your choices

  • In-app account deletion is available to every user. Deleting your account removes your authentication record and household membership.
  • When the last parent in a household deletes their account, the household's data — child profiles, schedules, plans, tasks, timesheets, and all photos — is deleted.
  • Deleted data may persist in encrypted backups for a limited period (up to 30 days) before being purged.
  • You may also access, correct, or delete specific content directly in the app, or contact us at privacy@sembli.co.
  • Push notifications can be disabled in iOS Settings at any time.
  • U.S. state privacy laws may grant additional rights (access, deletion, correction, non-discrimination); contact us to exercise them.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access is restricted per household: nannies and parents see only their own household's data, and photos are private to the household. Authentication uses Sign in with Apple and single-use invite tokens; no passwords are stored. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected users and regulators of breaches as required by law.

10. Changes to this policy

We will post updates here and, for material changes, notify users in the app before the change takes effect.

11. Contact
